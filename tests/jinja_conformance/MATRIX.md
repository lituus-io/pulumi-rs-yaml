Generated by `crates/pulumi-rs-yaml-core/tests/jinja_conformance.rs` from the
conformance corpus, reference Jinja2 3.1.6's registry and the engine's own; do not
edit. Regenerate with `JINJA_CONFORMANCE_BLESS=1 cargo test -p pulumi-rs-yaml-core
--test jinja_conformance`.

**partial** = available, with a listed divergence; **unsupported** = refused;
**extension** = pulumi-rs-yaml only. Reasons are in `DIVERGENCES.md`.
